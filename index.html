About

Direction with intent.

I'm a video director working across commercial, narrative and music video formats. My focus is on building a clear visual language for every project — from concept and shot design through to the final color pass — so that every frame is doing something on purpose.

Over the past several years I've collaborated with brands, artists and independent producers to shape ideas into films that hold attention and leave an impression.
